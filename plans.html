Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Do I need to visit a clinic?

No. Your sample kit comes to your home and goes back by post. Your nutritionist, plan, food and tracking all live in the app — so it fits around your life, not the other way round.

Is my plan really personalised?

Yes. Your plan is built from your own report by a real nutritionist — not pulled off a shelf. As you re-test, it adapts to your new numbers, so it keeps fitting you.

Will I speak to a real person?

Always. A qualified nutritionist is assigned to you and stays with you — reviewing your progress, answering your questions, and adjusting your plan as you go.

How soon will I see results?

Many people start to feel a difference within the first few weeks, and your monthly progress checks make it visible. Lasting change builds over time — and your plan keeps improving with you.
