Our name, our reason

Anvayya means woven together.

In Sanskrit, anvayya means coherent, connected, woven together. It's the whole idea behind us: your health isn't a stack of separate things — it's one connected story, and it should be built on you.

Why we exist

Your health should be built on your biology — not a generic plan.

For too long, looking after your health has meant guessing. A diet that worked for someone else. An app that knows nothing about you. Advice that's the same for everyone. Meanwhile, the answers were always inside your own body — in your gut, in your metabolism — waiting to be read.

We started Anvayya to change that. To read your real biology, build everything around it, and walk with you while it works. So you stop guessing, and start understanding.

Science & people

Real science. Real people. Beside you.

The technology reads your biology with precision. But it's our clinical team that turns those numbers into a plan you can actually live — and stays with you as you follow it.

Grounded in science

Your plan starts from what your samples actually show about your gut and metabolism — measured, not assumed.

An in-house clinical lead

A qualified clinical nutritionist leads the care, reads your report personally, and shapes your plan around your biology.

A team that stays

Nutritionist, trainer and support — assigned to you, knowing your numbers, adjusting as you progress.
